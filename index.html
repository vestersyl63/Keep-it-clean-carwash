Our Story

Crafted with Passion

Under new ownership since November 2021, Keep It Clean Car Wash & Detail has been transformed into Petersburg's premier destination for automotive rejuvenation. We don't just clean cars; we restore them to better-than-showroom condition.

Led by industry professionals Raquel and Eric, our team combines old-school craftsmanship with modern techniques. Every vehicle receives the meticulous attention typically reserved for exotic automobiles.

We accommodate special requests because we understand that your vehicle is an investment, a tool, and often a source of pride. Whether it's preparing for a car show, maintaining a daily driver, or recovering from a road trip, we treat every job as if it were our own.
